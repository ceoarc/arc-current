ARC Current is a blog on the second-life battery market, published by ARC (Adaptive Research Corp.).

We plan to publish once or twice a week on topics including:

- Market trends in second-life batteries, in Korea and worldwide
- Technology for battery remanufacturing and energy storage systems (ESS)
- Policy, regulation, and certification updates

All posts are available in both Korean and English. Stay tuned.
